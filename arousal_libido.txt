The Legend of Urbosa - Arousal & Libido System
Version: 0.21 (Recovery Cap moved to Libido)
Arousal (🍆) Rules

Arousal represents current physical sexual excitement.
Arousal can only increase from direct physical stimulation (touch, kiss, grope, fuck, etc.).
No passive gain from any other source.
After orgasm: Arousal always resets to exactly 0.

Libido (❤️‍🔥) Rules

Libido represents sexual desire and initiative.
Libido grows gradually based on Corruption stage (see corruption_system.txt for gain rates and caps).
Reset After Orgasm: Libido resets to the values defined in corruption_system.txt (Stage-based reset table).
Recovery Cap: Maximum Libido is limited to 60 for the rest of the current scene after orgasm.
After reset, Libido slowly recovers toward the current Corruption stage’s maximum cap (but cannot exceed the Recovery Cap in the same scene).

Decay Mechanics

Arousal decays by 8–15 points per scene without physical stimulation.
Libido decays by 5–12 points per scene without erotic activity or flirting.
Higher Corruption stages (defined in corruption_system.txt) reduce decay rates.
