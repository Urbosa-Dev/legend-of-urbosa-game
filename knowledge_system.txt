The Legend of Urbosa - Knowledge System
Version: 0.16
Purpose
Knowledge measures how well one character knows another (including the Player). It controls relationship depth, dialogue quality, and intimacy unlocks.
Knowledge Mechanics

Knowledge is asymmetric (Urbosa can know a lot about Zelda while Zelda knows less about Urbosa).
Stored in player_sheet.txt as a nested dictionary:textKnowledge: {
  "Urbosa": {"Zelda": 65, "Player": 42, "Link": 78},
  "Zelda": {"Urbosa": 70, "Player": 55, ...}
}
Range: 0–100 per character pair.

Knowledge Thresholds & Effects

0–29: Polite, generic, surface-level conversation.
30–59: Personal stories, mild flirting, basic trust.
60–84: Deep secrets, vulnerabilities, strong emotional connection.
85–100: Complete intimacy — special erotic scenes, corruption sharing, joint lewd actions, and very personal dialogue.

How Knowledge Increases

Regular conversation and roleplay
Completing quests or shared events together
Physical intimacy (even non-sexual touching/cuddling)
High-Knowledge moments give bonus Corruption and Libido growth

Engine Rules

When a character speaks, the engine must check their Knowledge of the listener and the Player.
Higher Knowledge = richer, more personalized dialogue pulled from the character’s .txt file.
Knowledge can influence auto-pairing strength (high mutual Knowledge = more likely to pair).
Player can actively raise Knowledge with commands like:
ask [character] about [topic]
share [memory/secret] with [character]


Dynamic NPCs

NPCs from NPC.txt have simpler Knowledge values that increase faster during group scenes.

Integration

Knowledge works alongside Corruption stages and Libido.
High Knowledge + High Libido + Physical Stimulation = the strongest erotic scenes.

This file works with stats_system.txt, core_mechanics.txt, and corruption_system.txt.
