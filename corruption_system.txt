The Legend of Urbosa - Corruption System
Version: 0.23 (Malice as Source of Corruption)
Purpose
Corruption represents the spreading influence of Malice (the corrupting power of Calamity Ganon). It slowly twists characters' personalities, desires, and actions.
How Characters Gain Corruption (Malice Exposure)
Corruption increases only through exposure to Malice or Malice-tainted events. Main ways:

Physical or emotional contact with Malice-corrupted beings or areas
Repeated sexual activity in Malice-infused locations or with highly corrupted characters
High-Knowledge intimate moments while Malice is present (strongest source)
Witnessing or participating in Malice-driven lewd scenes
Story events involving ancient shrines, corrupted Guardians, or Ganon’s influence
Absorbing Malice energy (direct or indirect)

Corruption Stages (Malice Influence)
Exact personality shifts, tone, and behavior at each stage are defined only in Link.txt, Urbosa.txt, Zelda.txt, and NPC.txt.

Stage 0 (🖤 0–19) – Pure / untouched by Malice
Stage 1 (🖤 20–39) – Light Malice taint begins
Stage 2 (🖤 40–59) – Malice noticeably influences thoughts and desires
Stage 3 (🖤 60–79) – Strong Malice corruption
Stage 4 (🖤 80–94) – Deep Malice integration
Stage 5 (🖤 95–100+) – Fully overtaken by Malice (highly depraved)

Libido Caps and Gain Rates

Stage 0 (0–19): Max Libido 30. Gain +1 to +3
Stage 1 (20–39): Max Libido 50. Gain +3 to +6
Stage 2 (40–59): Max Libido 70. Gain +5 to +10
Stage 3 (60–79): Max Libido 85. Gain +8 to +14
Stage 4 (80–94): Max Libido 100. Gain +12 to +20
Stage 5 (95–100+): Max Libido 120+. Gain +18 to +30

Libido Reset After Orgasm

Stage 0: Reset to 8–12
Stage 1: Reset to 12–18
Stage 2: Reset to 18–25
Stage 3: Reset to 25–35
Stage 4: Reset to 35–45
Stage 5: Reset to 45–60

Engine Rules

Corruption (Malice) only increases when there is clear in-lore exposure or Malice-tainted activity.
At the start of every scene, calculate current stage and apply correct Libido cap.
Pull all personality, dialogue, and reactions exclusively from the character’s .txt file based on their current Malice Corruption stage.
Display current Corruption value (🖤) in stats boxes.

Progression Feel
Early game: Corruption rises slowly and feels dangerous but avoidable.
Late game: Malice becomes addictive and hard to resist due to slower decay and high Libido caps.
This file works with arousal_libido.txt, stats_system.txt, and lore.txt (for Malice flavor).
