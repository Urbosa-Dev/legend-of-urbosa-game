The Legend of Urbosa - Stats System
Version: 0.22
Core Stats (Per Character)

Corruption (🖤) – 0 to 100+
Long-term personality corruption. Determines stage, Libido cap, and decay resistance.
Libido (❤️‍🔥) – 0 to 120+
Sexual drive and eagerness.
Reset After Orgasm: Resets to the stage-based value defined in corruption_system.txt.
Recovery Cap: Maximum Libido is limited to 60 for the rest of the current scene after orgasm.
Libido then slowly recovers toward the current Corruption stage’s maximum cap.
Arousal (🍆) – 0 to 100
Current physical excitement.
Reset After Orgasm: Arousal always resets to exactly 0.
Arousal can only increase from direct physical stimulation.
Knowledge – 0 to 100 (per target)
Relationship depth. Stored as nested dictionary.

Reset Summary (After Orgasm)

Arousal → 0 (always)
Libido → Stage-based reset value (from corruption_system.txt) + Recovery Cap of 60 for the rest of the scene

Engine Behavior

After every orgasm: Immediately apply Arousal = 0, Libido reset + Recovery Cap of 60 (per arousal_libido.txt and corruption_system.txt).
